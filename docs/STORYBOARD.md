## 04 — ONBOARD EMPLOYEE 001 (implemented)

1. **Answer:** “IF AI IS AN EMPLOYEE…” resolves into “ONBOARD IT LIKE ONE.”
2. **Readiness gap:** Employee 001 is capable but organizationally not ready; Culture, Constitution and Impact appear locked.
3. **Culture:** the audience supplies who, ambition, and what good looks like. Context visibly enters the entity and Culture loads.
4. **Constitution:** Permission, Approval, Prohibition, Escalation and Data progressively construct a governed perimeter.
5. **Decision:** a fictional material issue prompts A Fix autonomously, B Investigate + recommend, or C Stop and wait. Each selection has its own consequence. B is preferred only for this scenario.
6. **Impact:** the first 30 days lead into Helpful, Knowledgeable and Thinks Outside the Box. The North Star returns.
7. **Performance:** Time Saved is crossed out as insufficient alone; quality, speed, insight, creativity, decision quality and human capacity form the broader model.
8. **Activation:** all modules resolve and Employee 001 changes from Capable to Active.
9. **Signature:** “WE DIDN'T CONFIGURE SOFTWARE. WE ONBOARDED A MEMBER OF THE WORKFORCE.”
10. **Cliffhanger:** the active entity begins monitoring and receives an anomaly signal. Scene 05 remains unrevealed.

3. Employee 001 gathers six evidence regions while the cognitive-state primitive advances.

5. Additional evidence is requested, and Market Context specialist 014 connects.
6. H1 market demand and H3 supply lose support; H2 conversion deterioration gains support.
7. “What evidence would prove me wrong?” triggers four falsification checks.
8. Synthesis separates change, likely cause, stable factors, €42M explained, and €18M unknown.
9. A four-part recommendation resolves with 78% confidence and visible uncertainty.
10. Human Decision Required offers three reversible A/B/C branches and consequences.
11. AI investigates, analyses, challenges, and recommends; the human judges, decides, and owns the outcome.
12. Human in the Loop is replaced by Human in Command.
13. Repetitive cognitive work moves to AI; judgment, accountability, relationships, courage, and leadership remain human.
14. The old work allocation transforms into an AI-native model and the Finance leadership statement.
15. Employee 001 returns with WORKING added to its maturity; the scene cuts on the abundance question.
