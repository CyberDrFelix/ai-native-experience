# Keynote master

## Identity

**AI NATIVE — The Experience**  
**Promise:** “Your Next Employee Might Be AI. Your Next Leader Must Be Ready.”  
Presenter and author: Felix Burcea

The initial format is a 60-minute virtual experience for a CFO organization in India. The product must remain reusable for executive education, corporate training, universities, conferences, and commercial workshops.

## Thesis

The audience does not merely hear about an AI employee. It helps create, onboard, govern, and work with **EMPLOYEE 001**.

Using AI → Becoming AI-native → AI employee → AI employee onboarding → AI autonomy → Human in Command → AI-native leadership → Human × AI

Core principles:

1. Do not simply use AI. Become AI-native.
2. Ask: “If AI existed on Day 1, would we design this process the same way?”
3. Onboard AI employees with Culture, Constitution, and Impact.
4. A great AI employee is helpful, knowledgeable, and able to think outside the box.
5. Human leaders retain purpose, ethics, judgment, accountability, and orchestration.
6. The future leader understands human and AI employees and blends them successfully.
7. Human in the Loop evolves toward **Human in Command**.
8. Theory informs. Experience transforms.

## Seven-scene arc

1. **BOOT:** the approved local Digital Felix production video delivers the complete Kannada → Hindi → English reveal, then hands cleanly to live Felix. The presenter starts playback explicitly; a static local fallback preserves the opening if media fails.
2. **THE GAP:** audience locates its maturity; reveal “USING AI ≠ BEING AI-NATIVE”; introduce the North Star question.
3. **CREATE EMPLOYEE 001:** chatbot → copilot → agent → AI employee. Capabilities: read, research, reason, plan, analyse, create, remember, monitor, act, communicate, collaborate. Close: “If this really is an employee, why do we onboard it like software?”
4. **ONBOARDING:** answer “If AI is an employee, onboard it like one.” Culture supplies context; Constitution establishes autonomy within boundaries through an audience decision; Impact defines first-30-day expectations and broader performance. End with activation, the workforce signature statement, and an incoming signal.
5. **CFO SIMULATION:** fictional monitoring scenario; forecast deviation −6.2%; Employee 001 investigates, analyses patterns, checks drivers, forms/tests three hypotheses, consults a specialist, assesses materiality; illustrative drivers, 82% confidence, recommended action, then human decision required. Learning: AI changes what the CFO spends time doing.
6. **THE AI-NATIVE LEADER:** answer the abundance question; move human work upward; evolve Human in the Loop into Human in Command; join human and AI workforce capabilities through leadership/orchestration; reveal the future-leader statement and Human × AI; end with “THEORY INFORMS. EXPERIENCE TRANSFORMS.”
7. **ACTION + BELIEF:** Monday Morning Challenge, one-process redesign commitment, final belief, optimistic human close, and thank-you.

## Timing intent

BOOT 4m · THE GAP 6m · CREATE 8m · ONBOARDING 12m · CFO SIMULATION 14m · AI-NATIVE LEADER 9.5m · ACTION + BELIEF flexible close. Timing is guidance; presenter control is beat-based.

## Digital Felix production story

Digital Felix plays the opening straight. After “Felix… over to you,” live Felix may say: “Well… that’s a slightly uncomfortable way to start my own presentation.” The twin’s unusually expressive hand gestures are authentic output from its training: Felix was instructed to be expressive during the avatar recording, and the system reproduced that behavior too aggressively.

The anecdote should stay conversational, then land the real point: **“AI learns from what we give it — including our mistakes.”** Use it to introduce the importance of context, training, demonstrated behavior, governance, and onboarding. This is a human reflection after the video, never an overlay or explanation inside the production media.
